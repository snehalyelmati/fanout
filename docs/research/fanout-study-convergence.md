# Fanout study convergence

The curated public synthesis is now the [Fanout research study](../STUDY.md), with [per-run summaries and static figures](../study/README.md).

Parallel Fanout remains the primary architecture. The study identifies conditional winning mechanisms and a modeled feasibility region; repeated validation across real task/model/provider combinations remains incomplete. Whole-task submission is a request-depth experiment, not a replacement product.

The September 2026 research-preview scope is repository source, a readable study report and static figures. Historical cohorts remain frozen, and no additional paid benchmark is required for this release. See the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) and [publication checks](../PUBLICATION.md).
